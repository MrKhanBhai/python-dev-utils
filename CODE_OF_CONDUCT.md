# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in python-dev-utils a respectful, welcoming, and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, constructive feedback.
- Focus on the work, not personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For project-related conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact options on the GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and respectfully as possible.
